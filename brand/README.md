# Quilt: logo guidelines

## 1. The logo
- **Idea:** the Q is pieced from four pastel patches, and its tail is a thread. The thread leaves the Q, waves
  gently just under "uilt" and ends in a sharp point, as if the word has just been sewn. Many pieces, one whole:
  which is what Quilt does for people and their AIs.
- **Construction:** the Q is a ring cut into four quarter patches, clockwise from 12 o'clock: peach, butter,
  mint, lilac. The thread is one third of the ring's weight and ends in a tapered point.
- **Versions:**
  - `logo/quilt-horizontal.svg`: primary. The pieced Q, then "uilt" (Poppins SemiBold, outlined), with the thread.
  - `logo/quilt-symbol.svg`: the Q alone, with a short curl of thread.
  - `logo/quilt-icon.svg`: the symbol on a blush tile: the app icon. `quilt-icon-maskable.svg` is full bleed with
    the symbol inside the safe zone, for Android and PWA icons.
  - `logo/quilt-symbol-small.svg`: the small-size cut (one colour, no patches, a straight tail); used for 16–32 px
    and favicons.
  - `-dark` versions: for dark backgrounds (light wordmark, peach thread).
  - `one-colour/`: black, white and rust (`#C24F33`) versions for single-colour printing, embroidery and stamps.
  - `web/`: favicon.ico / favicon.svg, apple-touch-icon, 192/512 and maskable icons, `site.webmanifest`,
    and `head-snippet.html` to paste into a page's `<head>`.
- **Source:** every file here, and the desktop app's icon sources, is generated by `scripts/make-brand.cjs`
  (`npx electron scripts/make-brand.cjs`, then `npx electron desktop/make-icons.cjs`). The on-page mark is
  `web/lib/mark.js` (kept identical to `src/ui/mark.js`). Change the drawing there, not in the SVGs.

## 2. Clear space
Keep **half the Q's height** clear on every side. The zone scales with the logo.

## 3. Minimum size
| Version | Screen | Print |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal | 96 px wide | 25 mm wide |
| Symbol / icon (full detail) | 40 px | 10 mm |
| Below that | use `quilt-symbol-small.svg` / the favicon files, down to 16 px | 6 mm |

## 4. Colour: Sherbet
| Name | HEX | RGB | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Peach | `#FFB8A3` | 255, 184, 163 | First patch (top right); the thread on dark backgrounds |
| Butter | `#FFE08A` | 255, 224, 138 | Second patch (bottom right) |
| Mint | `#A8E6CF` | 168, 230, 207 | Third patch (bottom left) |
| Lilac | `#C9B8FF` | 201, 184, 255 | Fourth patch (top left) |
| Ink | `#2B2A38` | 43, 42, 56 | The wordmark, the thread, and text |
| Warm white | `#FFF8F3` | 255, 248, 243 | Page background |
| Blush | `#FDEEE6` | 253, 238, 230 | Icon tile, raised surfaces |
| Rust | `#C24F33` | 194, 79, 51 | Links and small accents; the one-colour mono version |

The patches always keep their places and order. **Approved pairs:** full colour on warm white, blush or white ·
dark version on ink · one-colour black on light · one-colour white on dark or on rust.
The pastels are too light for text: set text in ink, and use rust only for links and short labels.

## 5. Typography
- Wordmark and headlines: **Poppins SemiBold** (the wordmark is outlined, so don't retype it).
  Body text: Poppins Regular/Medium · code: IBM Plex Mono · web fallback: system-ui, sans-serif.
- Licences: Poppins and IBM Plex Mono are both SIL Open Font License, so they're free for logos and apps.

## 6. Don'ts
Don't recolour or reorder the patches · don't dash, straighten or thicken the thread · don't add a needle or other
ornament to its point · don't stretch, rotate or skew the logo · don't add shadows, outlines, gradients or
textures · don't move the thread away from the letters or change the space between the Q and "uilt" · don't set
the wordmark in live text.

## 7. Files
Masters are SVG. PNGs are exports. `presentation/` still shows the earlier pieced-block logo.
A trademark search is still needed before registering the mark.
